Maintained on the CERN Open Data Portal. Indexed here because these pages are
hard to find unless you already know they exist.

The 2010 datasets are the smallest CMS has released, which makes them the least
painful way to learn the Run 1 toolchain even if your eventual target is 2011
or 2012. Expect the environment setup to dominate your first day.
