Maintained on the CERN Open Data Portal. Indexed here.

The least well-known of the CMS open data releases, and the one people most
often do not realise exists. If your interest is quark-gluon plasma, jet
quenching, or collective flow, this is the door.

Assume nothing transfers automatically from a proton-proton workflow. Centrality
determination and the underlying event treatment are genuinely different.
