If your students do not code, and you do not want to spend three class
periods teaching them to, this is the shortest path from zero to a real
measurement made with LHC data.

The material has not been updated since 2017 and the repository has no active
maintainer, but the exercises still work as written: the data files are static
CSVs and spreadsheet software has not changed underneath them. It is listed
here because it is the lowest-barrier activity in the whole collection, and it
was until recently almost impossible to find.

Translations exist in Finnish, Swedish, German, Spanish, and Greek alongside
the English original. Translation quality varies and corrections are welcome.
