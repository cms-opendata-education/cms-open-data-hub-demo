A dictionary, and an excellent one. When you know the term you need — which jet
energy correction applies, what a given NanoAOD branch contains, how to treat a
particular object — this is where the answer lives.

It is not a tutorial and does not try to be. If you are at the stage of "I have
data and no idea what to do next," start from one of the getting-started
pathways instead and come back here when you hit a specific question.
